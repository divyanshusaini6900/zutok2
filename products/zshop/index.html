Everything inZShop.

Every feature below is already built into ZShop. Nothing to add later and no plugins to install.

Connect any store

Shopify through a custom app, WooCommerce or any platform with an API, or an in-house shop whose items live in the CRM. Every feature works the same on all three.

WhatsApp order updates

Order placed, packed, shipped and delivered, sent from your ZChat number. When a buyer replies, it lands in the same inbox.

COD confirmation

Ask cash-on-delivery buyers to confirm on WhatsApp, remind them automatically and tag confirmed orders. Nothing is cancelled unless you choose to.

Abandoned-cart recovery

Three reminders, after one hour, one day and three days, with a discount you set at each step. The first reminder carries none.

Courier tracking

Checks courier status for out-for-delivery, delivered and failed attempts, and learns your real delivery times, like “usually 3–5 days”.

Quiet hours & limits

Promotional messages respect quiet hours and a daily limit. Order updates always go out when they happen.

Buyers → CRM

Every customer becomes a CRM lead, matched on phone number only, so two people with the same name are never mixed up.

One do-not-contact list

When someone says “stop contacting me”, it applies on every channel, shared with ZChat and ZCall.

Automations & templates

Discounts, carts, messages and automations in one place, with Meta template mapping for every numbered blank.

Know the rules

We handle WhatsApp's rules for you.

WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of a customer's last message. Most order updates come later than that, so ZShop maps each one to an approved template and fills in the details.
