Everything in ZChat.

Every feature below is already built into ZChat. Nothing to add later and no plugins to install.

One unified inbox

WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Telegram in one place, with All, Mine and Unassigned views and Open, Pending and Resolved status.

AI sales agent

Add your products once. The agent asks each choice as a numbered list and replies with the price, order link and files from the one row that matches.

Your own columns & rows

Size, colour, paper, delivery time, brochure: any column, any number. Set each one as a choice, information, quantity, price, link or file.

Business knowledge

Timings, address, delivery areas, payment and return policy. The AI answers only from the facts you give it and never guesses.

Choose your AI

Run the agent on OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Vertex AI or your own model, with your own temperature and reply length.

Auto-assign backlog

Spread new and waiting chats across your team automatically so nothing sits unanswered.

WhatsApp broadcasting

Send Meta-approved templates to leads, contacts or a custom audience, and see sent, delivered and read numbers as they come in.

Template studio

Create and sync Text, Image, PDF, Video and Button templates for Marketing, Utility and Authentication, and track their approval status.

Comment → DM automation

Auto-reply to comments on Instagram and Facebook posts and reels, and send a private DM to everyone who comments.

Labels & quick replies

Tag conversations and answer the questions you get every day with saved replies.

Contacts → CRM leads

Every new chat creates a lead in Zutok CRM with its source, so sales can follow it up from one place.

Team reports

Conversations, resolution rate, average resolution time, load per channel and per agent.
