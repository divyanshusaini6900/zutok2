Zutok pricing in rupeesPay for what you actually use.

Zutok ZChat starts at ₹2,000/month, or ₹1,599/month billed yearly (₹19,200 a year). Zutok CRM costs ₹1,299 per user per month, ₹1,199 per user with 3 users and ₹999 per user with 5 or more users; billed yearly it's 20% less (₹1,039, ₹959 and ₹799 per user per month). ZShop and Zloya aren't sold separately: they're included free with ZChat Growth (₹5,000/month, or ₹3,999/month billed yearly) and Scale (₹10,000/month, or ₹7,499/month billed yearly). Prices exclude 18% GST, and there is no setup fee.
