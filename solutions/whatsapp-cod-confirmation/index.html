Zutok ZShop · COD confirmation

COD order confirmation on WhatsApp, before anything ships

Zutok ZShop asks cash-on-delivery buyers to confirm their order on WhatsApp before you ship it. If they don't reply, it reminds them automatically. Confirmed orders are tagged, and nothing is cancelled unless you switch that on. It works with Shopify, WooCommerce and in-house stores, from ZShop Starter at ₹1,299/month billed monthly (₹12,990/year), excl. 18% GST, with ZChat for WhatsApp delivery.

Why confirm COD orders before shipping?

Because a cash-on-delivery order costs you packing and courier charges before you've been paid anything. If the buyer didn't mean it or has changed their mind, you only find out when the parcel comes back as RTO.

A quick confirmation on WhatsApp gives you a signal before you commit stock and shipping. Orders the buyer confirms go out as normal, and orders without an answer can wait for your team to check them first.

How do I automatically confirm COD orders on WhatsApp before shipping?

Connect your store to ZShop and turn on COD confirmation. Each new COD order gets a WhatsApp message asking the buyer to confirm, a reminder follows if they don't answer, and confirmed orders are tagged.

The defaults are to remind after 4 hours and stop asking after 24 hours. Buyers who don't confirm are tagged rather than cancelled, so your team decides what happens to the order. If you'd rather cancel unanswered orders automatically, “Cancel if nobody replies” is there too, and it's off by default.

The message goes out from your ZChat number, for example with ✅ Confirm and ❌ Cancel buttons. If the buyer replies with a question instead, the reply lands in the same inbox for your team to answer.

Do COD confirmation messages need approved WhatsApp templates?

Usually, yes. WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. A buyer who ordered on your website hasn't messaged you on WhatsApp, so the confirmation goes out as an approved template that ZShop fills in.

You map your Meta templates once during setup, and ZShop shows you which order fields fill each numbered blank.
