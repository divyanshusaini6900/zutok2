About Zutok Softwares

Zutok Softwares makes Zutok CRM, an all-in-one CRM built for Indian businesses and priced in rupees. Three products plug into it: ZChat, a WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Telegram inbox with an AI sales agent; ZShop, which sends WhatsApp order updates, COD confirmations and abandoned-cart reminders for Shopify, WooCommerce and in-house stores; and Zloya, which runs loyalty points, VIP tiers, memberships and retention journeys for restaurants, cafés, salons and stores. Zutok CRM costs ₹1,299 per user per month (₹999 per user with 5 or more users) and ZChat plans start at ₹2,000/month, billed monthly and excluding 18% GST, and ZShop and Zloya come free with ZChat Growth and Scale.

What does Zutok Softwares make?

One CRM and three products that plug into it. Zutok CRM is priced per user, ZChat is sold as plans that each include CRM licenses, and ZShop and Zloya come free with ZChat Growth and Scale.

Zutok CRM

CRM software for small businesses

CRM software for small businesses in India: leads, customers, proposals, GST invoices, projects, HRM, payroll and inventory in one CRM. Chats from ZChat, orders from ZShop and guests from Zloya land in the same place.

₹1,299 per user per month billed monthly (₹999 per user with 5 or more users), excl. 18% GST.

Zutok ZChat

Omnichannel inbox + AI agent

WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Telegram land in one shared inbox. An AI agent answers in seconds, your team takes over when it matters, and every chat becomes a lead in your CRM.

Plans from ₹2,000/month billed monthly, excl. 18% GST.

Zutok ZShop

Shopify & WooCommerce WhatsApp integration

Connect Shopify, WooCommerce or your in-house counter. ZShop brings every order into the CRM, confirms cash-on-delivery on WhatsApp, recovers abandoned carts and keeps buyers updated until the parcel arrives.

Included free with ZChat Growth (₹5,000/month) and Scale (₹10,000/month), excl. 18% GST.

Zutok Zloya

Loyalty program software

Loyalty program software for restaurants, cafés, salons and stores. Award points at the counter, sell memberships, run automated win-back journeys and collect guest feedback and Google reviews.

Included free with ZChat Growth (₹5,000/month) and Scale (₹10,000/month), excl. 18% GST.

How does onboarding work?

You start with a free demo, then the Zutok team sets your account up with you on a call. During onboarding we help you import customers, leads and items from Excel or your old CRM, and connect your number to the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

  1. 01Book a demoWe look at how you sell today and show you the parts of Zutok that fit.

  3. 03Watch it runChats get answered, orders confirmed and guests come back, all in one CRM.

How is Zutok priced?

In Indian Rupees, excluding 18% GST, billed monthly or yearly. There is no setup fee. Monthly plans can be cancelled at the end of any month. Billed yearly, Zutok CRM costs 20% less, and yearly ZChat plans cost ₹19,200 for Starter, ₹47,988 for Growth or ₹95,988 for Scale a year.

  • Zutok ZChatfrom ₹2,000/month
  • Zutok CRM₹1,299 per user/month
  • Zutok ZShop and Zutok Zloyafree with ZChat Growth and Scale

Meta charges per template message at its published rates for India, billed separately from your Zutok plan and at cost. Meta's WhatsApp pricing page lists the current rates and the messages it doesn't charge for.

There is special pricing for non-profits, early-stage startups and chains with 10+ outlets. Ask during your demo.
