01 · The problem

Where do D2C brands lose sales on WhatsApp and Instagram?

A D2C sale in India often starts with a comment or DM asking for the price, moves to WhatsApp, and ends in a cash-on-delivery order that still has to be confirmed, packed, shipped and tracked. Each step tends to live in a different app, and each gap costs something: DMs nobody answered, carts left behind, COD parcels shipped to buyers who were never sure.

Fashion brands feel it most, because buyers want to know the colour, size and price before they commit, and they expect the answer in the same chat.

03 · Step by step

How does one order run from Instagram comment to doorstep?

This walk-through follows a single saree order. The products and prices are examples, not a customer's data.

  1. 1

    Comment

    A reel gets a “price?” comment

    ZChat replies publicly and sends the commenter a private DM with the price. You set the rule once, to trigger on keywords or on any comment.

  2. 2

    Chat

    The AI answers the questions

    The buyer asks whether the maroon Banarasi silk is available. The AI offers the colours as a numbered list, then replies with the price and order link from the matching catalogue row. With handoff on, a buyer who asks for a person moves to your team with the whole history.

  3. 3

    Order

    The order comes in from your store

    Whether it was placed on Shopify, WooCommerce or taken in-house, the order arrives in ZShop and the buyer becomes a CRM lead, matched on phone number only.

  4. 4

    COD

    COD is confirmed before you ship

    ZShop asks the buyer to confirm the cash-on-delivery order on WhatsApp, reminds them if they don't reply and tags confirmed orders. Nothing is cancelled unless you switch on auto-cancel.

  5. 5

    Shipping

    Updates follow the parcel

    Packed, shipped, out-for-delivery and delivered messages go out on their own. ZShop checks courier status and learns your real delivery times, so the shipped message can say something like “usually 3–5 days”.

  6. 6

    Carts

    Abandoned carts get three reminders

    Buyers who leave without paying get a reminder after one hour, one day and three days. The first reminder carries no discount, and you choose the discount on the second and third.
